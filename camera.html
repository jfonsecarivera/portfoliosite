Phone Case Camera
  • Overview

Collaborated with April Wang and Marjorie Antohi for Pedro Lopes' Intro to HCI at the University of Chicago.

In this project, we model a 3D phone case that allows users to take photos. We engineer three mechanisms, each of which provide haptic feedback. We have a zoom dial that stops at the minimum and maximum zoom; we have a four-mode dial, that snaps into place when you reach a new mode, and we have a button that takes a photo when you click down and snaps back into place.

Let’s make something cool!
