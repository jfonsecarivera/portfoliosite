Why I Stopped Reading News About Coronavirus

I am no stranger to Doom Scrolling. I am guilty of scrolling through r/coronavirus (I don't even want to link it) on Reddit. This was a great source of stress for me: I was counting every number, looking at infection rates across populations, observing hospital capacities, and to put it candidly, watching people die. I decided to cut back on coronavirus news, and found that my days were generally happier. At a certain point, though, I cut the news out all together. Why?

I Already Know What I Need To Do

I have come to realize that no coronavirus news will (or should) change my behavior. I should wear my mask and I should socially distance. What else do I need to know? If you've felt the stress of reading about coronavirus news, I personally recommend you try cutting it out. Seriously, you're not going to miss much.
