What the H*ll is the "Mark of the Beast"?

Mark of the what? I started doing some research. According to wikipedia:

The Classical Greek word charagma (χάραγμα), translated as mark (of the beast) in Revelation 13:16 can also mean any mark engraved, imprinted, or branded; stamped money, document, or coin.

The mark of the beast is interpreted differently across the four main views of Christian Eschatology...


Futurist view

Some fundamentalist Christian groups interpret the mark as a requirement for all commerce to mean that the mark might actually be an object with the function of a credit card, such as RFID microchip implants. Some of these groups believe the implantation of chips may be the imprinting of the mark of the beast, prophesied to be a requirement for all trade and a precursor to God's wrath.

The interpretations go on to explain that it's typically a mark on the hand or head, and as you can see, my microchip is in my hand.

I was surprised to learn that so many people knew about this Christian concept... I don't consider myself religious, but I went to a Catholic school and feel like I know a decent amount about Christianity... Maybe not, I guess.
