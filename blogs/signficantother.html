I Made A Mobile App for my Significant Other (And She Was Very Supportive)

Two days ago, I published an article: I Made A Mobile App for my Significant Other (And She Doesn't Use It). I tried to tell a story I found funny while marketing my app at the same time.

Fortunately, I reached the front page of HackerNews, and definitely marketed my app!

Unfortunately, I've gotten many misogynistic emails from people telling me things like "this is the way women are," "get used to it," etc..

I couldn't do it without her

The truth is, she does use it (although we did watch Space Jam that one night). She has probably used it more than anyone else. She used it when swiping on a movie heated her phone up, she used it when adding a friend broke the entire app, and she used it when nobody else did.

That's all, sorry hun! Look out for her upcoming article: "My Significant Other Slandered Me on the Internet to Market his App."
